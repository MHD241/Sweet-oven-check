Cakes on the stone

Welsh cakes, or pice ar y maen, are small, round and spiced with cinnamon, with sultanas through the middle. They are cooked on a griddle, not in an oven.

Ready to take away

Every pack is bagged and tied, ready to carry home or give to someone.

Allergens

Wheat (gluten)MilkEgg

Ingredients: self-raising wheat flour, butter, sultanas, sugar, egg, milk, cinnamon, salt. May contain traces of other allergens. If you have an allergy, ask us before you buy.

About The Sweet Oven

The Sweet Oven is a bakery in Dunfermline. We start with Welsh cakes, and we plan to add other sweet bakes over time.

Small batches

Everything is baked in small batches, so each cake gets the same attention.

Simple recipe

Flour, butter, sugar, sultanas, egg, milk and cinnamon, cooked on a griddle.

Clear allergen info
